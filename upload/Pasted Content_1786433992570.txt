SCHOLARIO-OS — FINAL ATTENDANCE ENTRY UX + WORKING-DAY HISTORY + OFFICIAL EXCEL-STYLE REPORT
================================================================================================

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT keep patching the current date-picker inside the Teachers & Employees
attendance entry screen.

The current date picker approach is making the page unnecessarily complicated.

We are changing the architecture of this screen.

The Teachers & Employees Attendance page should primarily be an
ATTENDANCE MARKING / SUBMISSION screen.

Historical attendance is accessed through a small Previous Working Day
navigation control, not through a large date picker.

Also, the official attendance report must NOT look like a dashboard/card UI.
It should look like an actual official school attendance sheet exported from
Excel/office software.

Do not blindly implement only the literal requirements below.
After implementing them, review the complete UX and make sensible premium,
logical improvements yourself without changing the intended workflow.

================================================================================================

Currently the screen has:

[ December 10th... ]

as a large date selector.

REMOVE THIS DATE PICKER FROM THE MAIN Teachers & Employees attendance screen.

Do NOT replace it with another large calendar.

The main screen should automatically be:

TODAY'S ATTENDANCE.

Example:

Today = 11 August 2026

The page opens directly on:

Teachers & Employees
11 August 2026

The user does not need to manually choose today's date.

This makes the page much more operational and much less cluttered.
