==================================================
1. DEFINE THE CORRECT PRODUCT MODEL
==================================================

SCHOLARIO-OS should conceptually support:

A. Regular Fee
B. Additional Collection
C. Application / Form
D. Optional connection between an Application and an Additional Collection

Think of them as separate but connectable objects.

--------------------------------------------------
A. REGULAR FEE
--------------------------------------------------

Examples:

- Tuition Fee
- Development Fee
- Transport Fee
- Annual Charges
- Examination Fee if configured as regular fee

These continue to work through the existing Fee Management / Fee Structures / Student Accounts system.

DO NOT disrupt this.

--------------------------------------------------
B. ADDITIONAL COLLECTION
--------------------------------------------------

An Additional Collection is a school-created money collection outside the normal recurring/class fee structure.

Examples:

- Educational Tour
- Robotics Workshop
- Annual Day Costume
- Sports Competition
- Olympiad Registration
- School Development Fund
- Exam Material
- ID Card Replacement
- Special Event
- Picnic
- Uniform / Activity Charges
- Any other one-time or special collection

A Principal must be able to create an Additional Collection directly.

It must NOT require an Application Form.

--------------------------------------------------
C. APPLICATION / FORM
--------------------------------------------------

An Application/Form is a structured submission or consent request.

Examples:

- Educational Tour Consent
- Sports Competition Registration
- Club Registration
- Student Activity Permission
- Medical Information Form
- Event Participation Form
- Transport Request
- Leave Application
- General School Application
- Parent Consent
- Any other school-defined form

Forms are not inherently financial.

A form may have:

- no payment
- an optional payment
- a required payment
- a linked Additional Collection

But the form itself should remain a separate concept.
