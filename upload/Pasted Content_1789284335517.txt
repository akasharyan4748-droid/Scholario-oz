SCHOLARIO-OS — STUDENT WORKSPACE
MODULE: FEES
FULL PRODUCTION-GRADE REDESIGN + FUNCTIONALITY UPGRADE

IMPORTANT:

You are NOT being asked to merely redesign the existing Fees page.

You are being asked to evolve Fees into a complete, production-grade,
secure, transparent and extremely easy-to-use financial experience
inside the Scholario Student Workspace.

The existing screenshot/page is a VISUAL REFERENCE and starting point.

Do not blindly preserve its limitations.

Study the existing application, architecture, database models,
authentication, permissions, School Settings, existing Student Workspace,
navigation patterns and previously implemented modules BEFORE making changes.

DO NOT break existing functionality.

DO NOT create fake backend functionality merely to make the UI look complete.

Everything that appears actionable must either:
1. work end-to-end, or
2. clearly communicate that the capability is not configured/enabled.

Never simulate successful payments, receipts, balances, messages or financial records.

========================================================
1. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
========================================================

Fees should feel like:

A premium digital school finance desk.

Not:

"a page showing ₹9,500".

The student/parent should be able to understand their financial position
within seconds.

The system should answer:

• What do I owe?
• What have I already paid?
• What is due now?
• What is due later?
• What exactly is each charge for?
• Was any concession/discount applied?
• When was the payment made?
• What payment method was used?
• Where is my receipt?
• What happens if I cannot pay online?
• Who can I contact?
• What is the status of my request?
• Is there any pending action?
• Can I safely raise a question/dispute?
• Can I download official documents?

The experience must maximize:

TRANSPARENCY
SECURITY
CLARITY
ACCOUNTABILITY
EASE OF USE

while minimizing:

TEXT
CLUTTER
CONFUSION
UNNECESSARY CLICKS
UNNECESSARY FORMS
REPEATED INFORMATION
